    Notes

    1. Data is the public Brazilian E-Commerce dataset Olist released on Kaggle: about 100k orders and 99,224 reviews from 2016 to 2018. Only the 40,950 reviews with written text were classified.
    2. The model is Qwen 2.5 7B served by Ollama on an RTX 4060, with answers limited to a JSON list of 12 theme names. It is wrong some of the time, and the counts here carry that error. The rules for unclear cases, like an order still not delivered after the deadline, are in the labelling guide.
    3. English versions of the example reviews come from an offline translation model and are sometimes clumsy. Switch to Portuguese to read the originals.
    4. "Value of orders" is the value of orders that drew a complaint. It shows how much business a problem touches, not how much money was lost.
    5. Repeat purchase only counts orders placed at least 180 days before the data ends, so every customer had time to come back.

    Questions or corrections are welcome: abhishekpatnaik.in. The code and every decision behind these numbers are on GitHub.
